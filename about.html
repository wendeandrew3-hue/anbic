ABOUT ANBIC INSURANCE AGENCY

We touch lives, with style.

ANBIC is a Nairobi insurance agency built on a simple belief: protection should never feel complicated. Our advisors arrange motor, health, life, home and business cover for individuals, families and growing businesses across Kenya.

OUR STORY

Founded on real advice.

ANBIC Insurance Agency was founded by Beryl Atieno Oduk, a finance specialist in insurance, pensions and retirement planning, and a licensed insurance intermediary registered with the Insurance Regulatory Authority. She built the agency around one idea: that every Kenyan deserves a financial advisor who explains the fine print, shops the market on their behalf, and picks up the phone when it matters.

Today the ANBIC team advises clients across Nairobi County and beyond, from a first car policy to corporate shields that keep employers compliant and their teams protected: WIBA, group life and personal accident cover.
